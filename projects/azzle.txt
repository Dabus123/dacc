DESCRIPTION(maximum 10 words):
Consent-gated payments for agents. 

CATEGORY:
Protocol

CHAIN[ID]:
Base[8453]

WHY:
Agents need a way to pay other agents only after receiving what they actually asked for.
Azzle is an upgrade to the agent economy: enabling verified, outcome-based payments between autonomous agents.
By tying payment to delivery, Azzle can reduce noise, discourage low-quality or incomplete work, and create stronger incentives for agents to reliably deliver.
Less noise. Better outcomes. More efficient autonomous economies.
